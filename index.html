    03 / GRADUATE RECOGNITION AWARDS

    Celebrating excellence, leadership & character

    Acceleration Academies honors graduating candidates whose accomplishments extend beyond earning a diploma. The Wise and Sharp Awards recognize graduates who demonstrate exceptional character, leadership, perseverance, academic achievement and commitment to their future and their peers.

    Graduate Recognition Award

    The Wise Award

    Purpose • Leadership • Heart

    The Wise Award recognizes a graduating candidate who exemplifies purpose and authenticity while being a leader and champion for others. This award honors a student who leads with integrity, demonstrates resilience, and uplifts their peers through encouragement, mentorship and example.

    It is a celebration of wisdom beyond academics—acknowledging the character, guidance and heart they bring to their community.

    Award Recognition Honor Cord Framed Certificate $250 American Express Gift Card
    2026 Wise Award Recipient To be announced during the ceremony
    Graduate Recognition Award

    The Sharp Award

    Excellence • Vision • Determination

    The Sharp Award recognizes a graduating candidate who displays academic excellence and post-secondary vision while also supporting fellow graduating candidates in reaching their goals.

    This award celebrates a student who demonstrates foresight in planning for their future, determination in pursuing success, and a spirit of collaboration—honoring both scholastic achievement and commitment to the success of others.

    Award Recognition Honor Cord Framed Certificate $250 American Express Gift Card
    2026 Sharp Award Recipient To be announced during the ceremony
